Support

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Write to mahmoud.reda.acc@gmail.com and you will get an answer. There is no ticket system, no chatbot and no form to fill in.

Who reads it, and when#

Own Hub was founded by Rahma Mohsen. It is published and developed by one person, Mahmoud Reda, and he reads this mailbox himself. There is no support team behind it, and we would rather say so than imply one.

Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 17:00 Cairo time. A reply within one working day. Not monitored at night.

If you write on a Friday or a Saturday, or after 17:00, your message is read on the next working day. Nothing about the app depends on that reply: it keeps working while you wait, because it never needed us.

If something in the app is hurting you or frightening you, do not wait for an email. Stop, and if you think it is an emergency contact emergency services or go to a hospital.

What to put in your message#

Four short lines make almost every problem solvable on the first reply:

Your phone
iPhone or Android, and which model if you know it.
The app version
Profile, then scroll to the bottom. If you cannot reach it, say roughly when you installed the app.
What you were doing
The screen you were on and what you tapped, in the order you tapped it.
What happened instead
What you expected, and what the app did. A screenshot is worth more than a paragraph.

Please do not send anything you would not want in an ordinary email. We never need your ID, your photograph, your measurements or your cycle history to answer a question, and we will never ask you for them. There is no password to reset, because the app has none.

The questions people actually write in about#

I deleted everything. Can you bring it back?#

No, and neither can anyone else. Deleting from Profile, then Your data, is immediate, and there is no restore and no grace period. We never had a copy, because the app has no server and sends us nothing, so there is nothing on our side to recover from. The same is true if you lose the phone or uninstall the app.

I am changing phone. Does my history come with me?#

Not from us. Your logs live in the app’s own private storage on the handset, and Own Hub has no account and no cloud backup, so there is nothing for us to move. A full device-to-device transfer or a full phone backup restored onto the new handset may carry it across, but that is your phone’s own feature and we cannot promise what it does. There is no export in this version, and the app says so rather than offering a button that would produce nothing.

Where do I sign in? Where is the community?#

There is nothing to sign in to. This version has no account, no password and no phone number, and it opens straight onto your own Home. There is no community, no coaching and no assistant in it either: nothing in the app connects you to another person, and nothing in it can be bought. If you were expecting one of those, you were reading about a later version that is not out.

How do I switch between Arabic and English?#

Profile, then Language. Pick the one you want and the whole app changes with it, right to left included. The setting stays after you close the app, and you can change it as often as you like.

What do you know about me?#

Nothing at all. The app has no network code in it, the released Android build does not even ask for permission to use the internet, and there is no analytics, crash reporting or advertising library anywhere in it. We could not tell you how many women use the app beyond what Apple and Google count for their own stores. The Privacy Policy explains why you can check that for yourself rather than take our word for it.
