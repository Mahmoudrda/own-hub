Coach Terms

Version: 1 (coach-terms-v1)
Effective date: 23 October 2026
Last updated: 23 October 2026
Applies to: women who apply to coach on Own Hub, in the app.

If the English and Arabic versions of these Terms differ, the Arabic version applies.

You accept these terms in the last step of the coach application. They sit beside the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy, which still apply to you as a member.

1. Reviewed coaches only#

  • Only a verified member can apply, and only a coach whom Own Hub’s woman reviewer has approved is shown to members.
  • The reviewer may approve, ask you for changes, or refuse, and tells you why. After a refusal you can apply again after 30 days.
  • Approval means your portfolio can be shown to members. Nothing is shown before it.

2. Accurate information#

  • Everything in your application and portfolio must be true: your name, your education, your certificates, your years of experience and your feedback.
  • Certificates and documents must be your own and unaltered.
  • Keep your portfolio up to date. We may remove a portfolio that is not accurate.

3. Your clients’ photos and words#

  • Add a before and after photo, or a screenshot of a client’s message, only with that woman’s written consent. You confirm in the app that you have it. A clear message from her is enough. Keep it, and show it to us if we ask.
  • Own Hub’s woman reviewer checks every photo and screenshot before members see it. When she approves one, we keep a re-encoded copy with no location or other metadata and delete the original. Verified members then see the approved items on your portfolio. You can remove an item yourself, and everything is deleted with your account.
  • You may also add photos of yourself or your work, which are checked the same way.
  • If a client withdraws her consent, tell us and we delete the photos.

4. What you may coach#

  • You coach as an independent professional. You are not Own Hub’s employee.
  • General fitness and healthy eating only.
  • Do not diagnose or treat any condition, prescribe a medical diet or recommend supplements.
  • If a woman is pregnant or has recently given birth, or you think she may have an eating disorder, refer her to a doctor.

5. Keep it inside Own Hub#

  • Do not put payment details or ways to reach you outside Own Hub (a phone number, a messaging account, a social media page) in your portfolio.
  • Do not ask a member to pay you or to talk to you outside Own Hub.

6. What members see, and prices#

  • Members see the public portfolio described in the Privacy Policy, section 3.15, including the photos the reviewer approved, and nothing else from your application.
  • While you are a coach, you let Own Hub show your approved portfolio content to members in the app.
  • In this version the price members see includes Own Hub’s service fee, and coaching is free for now: nobody can pay. Before payments open you will be asked to accept updated coach terms that set out prices, Own Hub’s share and payouts.
  • When a member joins you, you can read the form she filled in only while the coaching lasts, and you may use it only to coach her. Keep her answers, including her health answers, confidential, and use them only to coach her. When her coaching ends, delete any notes of your own about her. Messages with her stay inside Own Hub.

7. Reports, hiding and suspension#

  • Any member can report a portfolio.
  • Own Hub may hide or suspend a portfolio after a report, or if it breaks these terms. We tell you why and let you reply, unless the law, an order from an authority or someone’s safety prevents it.

8. Stopping#

  • You can withdraw an application, or stop being a coach, at any time. Write to support@own-hub.app, or delete your account in the app, from Profile, then Your data.
  • Deleting your account removes your application, its documents and your portfolio, as the Privacy Policy describes.

9. Payments, later#

Payments are not part of this version. If they are offered later, they will come with their own terms, shown to you before anything is charged, and you will be asked to accept them first.

10. Changes, law and contact#

  • If we change these terms in a way that matters, we ask you to accept them in the app. Other changes are published here.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt, and disputes go to the competent courts in Cairo, including the Cairo Economic Court for the matters the law assigns to it. This does not take away your right to bring a claim before any other Egyptian court the law allows you to use.
  • Contact: support@own-hub.app · Mahmoud Reda, Egypt
