Coach Terms

Version: 1 (coach-terms-v1)
Effective date: 23 October 2026
Last updated: 28 October 2026
Applies to: women who apply to coach on Own Hub, in the app.

If the English and Arabic versions of these Terms differ, the Arabic version applies.

You accept these terms in the last step of the coach application. They sit beside the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy, which still apply to you as a member.

1. Reviewed coaches only#

  • Only a verified member can apply, and only a coach whom Own Hub’s woman reviewer has approved is shown to members.
  • The reviewer may approve, ask you for changes, or refuse, and tells you why. After a refusal you can apply again after 30 days.
  • Approval means your portfolio can be shown to members. Nothing is shown before it.

2. Accurate information#

  • Everything in your application and portfolio must be true: your name, your education, your certificates, your years of experience and your feedback.
  • Certificates and documents must be your own and unaltered.
  • Keep your portfolio up to date. We may remove a portfolio that is not accurate.

3. Your clients’ photos and words#

  • Add a before and after photo, or a screenshot of a client’s message, only with that woman’s written consent. You confirm in the app that you have it. A clear message from her is enough. Keep it, and show it to us if we ask.
  • Own Hub’s woman reviewer checks every photo and screenshot before members see it. When she approves one, we keep a re-encoded copy with no location or other metadata and delete the original. Verified members then see the approved items on your portfolio. You can remove an item yourself, and everything is deleted with your account.
  • You may also add photos of yourself or your work, which are checked the same way.
  • If a client withdraws her consent, tell us and we delete the photos.

4. What you may coach#

  • You coach as an independent professional. You are not Own Hub’s employee.
  • General fitness and healthy eating only.
  • Do not diagnose or treat any condition, prescribe a medical diet or recommend supplements.
  • If a woman is pregnant or has recently given birth, or you think she may have an eating disorder, refer her to a doctor.

5. Keep it inside Own Hub#

  • Do not put ways to reach you outside Own Hub (a phone number, a messaging account, a social media page) in your portfolio. Your InstaPay address is set only in the payments setting of the app.
  • Do not move a member you met through Own Hub away from it, to coach her or to take payment from her outside Own Hub’s InstaPay steps, to avoid Own Hub’s rules or its fee. A client you brought with your own invite code is covered by section 9, Stay on Own Hub, in the same way: it does not apply to her.

6. What members see, and prices#

  • Members see the public portfolio described in the Privacy Policy, section 3.15, including the photos the reviewer approved, and nothing else from your application.
  • While you are a coach, you let Own Hub show your approved portfolio content to members in the app.
  • The price you set is the price members see and pay, with nothing added on top except a few piasters that make the amount match only that payment; those go to you. Members pay you directly by InstaPay. Own Hub charges you a fee, currently 15 percent of the package price for a client who found you in the app, and 8 percent for a client you brought with your own invite code. The rate in force when a payment’s access starts is the rate for that payment (section 9).
  • When a member joins you, you can read the form she filled in only while the coaching lasts, and you may use it only to coach her. Keep her answers, including her health answers, confidential, and use them only to coach her. When her coaching ends, delete any notes of your own about her. Messages with her stay inside Own Hub.

7. Reports, hiding and suspension#

  • Any member can report a portfolio.
  • Own Hub may hide or suspend a portfolio after a report, or if it breaks these terms. We tell you why and let you reply, unless the law, an order from an authority or someone’s safety prevents it.

8. Stopping#

  • You can withdraw an application, or stop being a coach, at any time. Write to support@own-hub.app, or delete your account in the app, from Profile, then Your data.
  • Deleting your account removes your application, its documents and your portfolio, as the Privacy Policy describes.

9. Payments by InstaPay#

  • You are paid directly, and you are the seller. A member pays your own InstaPay address from her own bank app. Own Hub never holds, routes, initiates or accesses that money. Own Hub is not a bank or a payment provider and is not a party to your agreement with the member. You are responsible for your own service and for refunds. Keep your address and account name exactly as your bank shows them. A new address takes effect after 24 hours, needs a recent sign-in (a code sent to your phone), and the members with a payment waiting are told.
  • Confirm honestly. When a member sends proof, check your own bank for the exact amount and the transaction number, then confirm, or say it has not arrived and attach your bank’s list for that day. No check can prove a payment; you are the check. You get a reminder after 48 hours. If you do not answer in 3 days, the payment goes to Own Hub staff to decide. A member whose proof passed the automatic checks may already have short access while you check.
  • Strikes and suspension. If the bank list you attach shows the exact amount or the transaction number, your claim that you were not paid is refused at once. That is not a strike. A strike comes only when a person at Own Hub decides, with a written reason, that a payment you said had not arrived was paid. Two strikes within 90 days flag your account. So does a dispute rate: at least 10 payments, of which 10 percent or more are disputes you lost (the numbers can be changed by Own Hub). A flagged account is reviewed by two different people at Own Hub, each with a written reason, and only then can your ability to take new payments be suspended. A suspension stops new payments. It does not by itself end coachings that are already running. There is no step to reply before you are flagged and no appeal procedure; you can write to support@own-hub.app and ask a person to look again.
  • The fee. The rate is read when a payment’s access starts and then kept on that payment: currently 15 percent for a client who found you in the app and 8 percent for a client you brought with your own invite code. It is worked out on the package price, not on the extra piasters, and rounded down. It is lowered if you accept a lower amount in writing in the app (at most 20 percent lower) and after a refund, and it is waived if the payment turns out not to have been paid. It is owed from the moment the client’s access starts, which can be short access before you confirm, not when you tap confirm. You get a monthly statement for the month before. If a statement is still unpaid 30 days after it is made, you cannot take new clients; current clients are not touched. You pay Own Hub by InstaPay and record the transfer; nothing is debited automatically. Own Hub keeps the statements for 5 years.
  • Refunds. A member can ask for a refund in the app, once for a payment, within 14 days of when her access began (when you confirmed the payment, or Own Hub decided it). You have 3 days to answer. You can send the money back by InstaPay and record it in the app (the transfer number, the amount and the time), or decline and give a reason: sessions delivered, outside the terms, or other. If you decline, or you are silent for 3 days, a person at Own Hub decides, and can decide that a refund is owed. Then you must send it back and record it. Own Hub does not send refund money. If you ignore a staff decision on a refund, staff may suspend your ability to take payments, and two people must agree.
  • Stay on Own Hub. While you coach a client you met through Own Hub, and for 12 months after the last coaching on Own Hub ends, you may not coach her or take payment from her outside Own Hub. This does not apply to clients you brought with your own invite code. For a confirmed breach Own Hub may warn you and charge 3 months of Own Hub’s share, a fair estimate of the fee it lost; for a second breach it may suspend you.
  • Tax. You are responsible for your own tax status and for any invoice you need to issue. A payment record from Own Hub is not a tax invoice.
  • Records. Payment records and the evidence in a dispute are kept, and fraud may be reported to the authorities. The Privacy Policy, section 3.18, says what is kept and for how long.

10. Changes, law and contact#

  • If we change these terms in a way that matters, we ask you to accept them in the app. Other changes are published here.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt, and disputes go to the competent courts in Cairo, including the Cairo Economic Court for the matters the law assigns to it. This does not take away your right to bring a claim before any other Egyptian court the law allows you to use.
