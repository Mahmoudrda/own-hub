Coach Agreement

Version: Coach agreement version 2, 28 October 2026
Applies to: every woman who coaches on Own Hub.
Sits beside: the Coach Terms (version 2).

If the English and Arabic versions of this agreement differ, the Arabic version applies.

This is the agreement between Own Hub and you, the coach. You agree to it in the app. Then you print it, sign it by hand with your full name, national ID number and date, and upload a photo or scan of the signed copy in the app. Own Hub staff check the signed copy before you can take new clients.

To print this page or save it as a PDF, use your browser’s Print command. The header, footer and menus are left out of the printed copy, and the last page has the signature block. A PDF of this page is also published at /coach-agreement.pdf.

1. Who the parties are#

  • Own Hub is the app and service published by Mahmoud Reda, Egypt (“Own Hub”).
  • The coach is the woman whose name, national ID number and signature are in the signature block at the end (“you”).
  • You coach as an independent professional. You are not Own Hub’s employee, partner or agent, and you cannot make a promise or sign a contract in Own Hub’s name. Own Hub does not act as your agent or represent you in your dealings with clients.
  • This agreement works together with the Coach Terms. If they differ on the commission, bills and the hold, the marketing videos, contact with clients or the 12-month rule, this agreement applies.

2. Own Hub’s commission#

  • For each payment a client makes to you for a package, you owe Own Hub a commission on the package price you receive: 15 percent for a client who found you through the app, and 7.5 percent for a client you brought with your own invite code.
  • Which rate applies is set once, when the coaching between you and that client first starts, and it does not change afterwards, also when she renews.
  • The commission is worked out on the package price, not on the few extra piasters that make the amount match one payment, and it is rounded down. After a refund it is worked out on what you kept. It is waived if a payment turns out not to have been paid, and it is lowered if you accept a lower amount in writing in the app, as the Coach Terms explain.
  • The commission is owed from the moment the client’s access starts, which can be short access before you confirm her payment.
  • If the law requires a tax to be added to Own Hub’s commission, Own Hub shows it on the bill. You are responsible for your own tax status and for any invoice you must issue to a client.

3. Paying the commission#

  • You pay the commission to Own Hub’s InstaPay account within 3 days of receiving the client’s payment. The account is shown on the bill in the app.
  • The app bills each payment separately, shows the amount and the due date, and sends you reminders before and on the due date.
  • After you pay, record the transfer in the app. Own Hub staff confirm it. Nothing is debited from you automatically, and you may not subtract the commission from anything else you owe or are owed.
  • The bill is a record that you owe the commission. It is not a tax invoice.

4. If a bill is not paid: the hold#

  • If a bill is still unpaid 7 days after it became owed, your coach account goes on hold. A hold is not a deletion.
  • While your account is on hold you can read everything in it, and you can record a payment of a bill. You cannot take new clients, send messages, share plans or schedule sessions. You are not shown in Find a coach.
  • Your clients keep their plans and can still read their chat. They see a neutral line that you are unavailable for now. Their coaching end dates move later by the length of the hold, so they do not lose days they paid for.
  • The hold ends automatically when Own Hub staff confirm the payment of the unpaid bill. If more than one bill is past its 7 days, all of them must be confirmed.
  • A hold does not cancel what you owe, and it does not stop the other duties in this agreement.

5. Two marketing videos a month#

  • The duty. Each calendar month you make 2 short videos for Own Hub and upload them in the app.
  • Length. Each video is at least 30 seconds and at most 3 minutes, filmed in good light with clear sound, in Egyptian Arabic or English.
  • Topics. A short workout or movement tip, a general healthy eating idea, a common myth about training, how coaching with you works, or a short introduction of yourself. General fitness and healthy eating only, as in the Coach Terms: no diagnosing, no medical diet, no supplements.
  • No client in any video. No client may appear, speak or be named, and you may not show a client’s message, photo, plan or result.
  • Women-only. Only women appear in your videos, and everyone who appears is an adult who agreed to be filmed. Film in a private or women-only place. Nobody films or uploads a video of a woman who did not agree.
  • Own rights. The video is yours. It must not use music, pictures or other things you have no right to use, and it may not advertise another brand or service.
  • Staff approval. Own Hub staff watch each video before it is used. They approve it or tell you why not, and you may send a new one. Only approved videos count towards the 2. Own Hub counts the videos each month. If fewer than 2 are approved in a month, Own Hub may remind you, and if it keeps happening Own Hub may treat it as a breach after written notice (section 11).
  • Licence. You give Own Hub a free, non-exclusive licence to use each approved video, with your name, voice and image in it, on Own Hub’s app, website and social media accounts and in its advertising. Own Hub may cut it, add captions, translate it and add its own branding, but may not change what you say or mean. Own Hub does not sell your videos to others. If you ask in writing, Own Hub stops new use of a video within 30 days; copies already published or paid for in an advertisement may stay until they end.
  • You keep your videos. The licence is non-exclusive. You keep your rights and may go on using your own videos, including the same ones, anywhere you like.

6. Clients stay inside Own Hub#

  • Never ask for contact details. You never collect, and never ask a client for, her phone number, email address or social media accounts, or any other way to reach her outside Own Hub. If she offers them, do not take them, tell her to use the app, and tell Own Hub.
  • Chat only in the app. You talk to clients only in Own Hub’s in-app chat. Do not put ways to reach you outside Own Hub in a message, a plan, a video or your portfolio.
  • Confidentiality. What a client tells you or sends you (her answers, her health answers, her photos and measurements, her messages) is confidential. Use it only to coach her through Own Hub. Do not share it with anyone, post it, or keep it after her coaching ends. This duty does not end when the agreement ends.
  • Follow the personal data protection law, Law 151 of 2020, in everything you do with a client’s data.

7. No dealing outside Own Hub for 12 months#

  • The rule. For a client you met through Own Hub, while you coach her and for 12 months after the last coaching between you on Own Hub ends, you may not coach her, contact her to coach her, or take payment from her outside Own Hub.
  • Who it does not cover. A client you brought with your own invite code is not covered by this section. Nothing here stops you coaching or charging anyone else, anywhere.
  • The way out: a conversion fee. If you and a client want to continue outside Own Hub, ask Own Hub in writing first. If Own Hub agrees in writing, you pay a conversion fee equal to 3 months of Own Hub’s commission on that client: the commission rate that applied to her, on her last monthly package price, times 3. Once you have paid it, the 12-month rule no longer applies to her.
  • If you break the rule. If you deal with a client outside Own Hub without paying the conversion fee first, you owe Own Hub the same amount as agreed compensation. You and Own Hub agree this is a fair estimate of the commission Own Hub loses, and that Own Hub does not have to prove its loss.
  • How it is decided. Own Hub staff decide, from evidence such as a client’s report, chat content or your own statement, after you have had a chance to answer, and give you a written reason.
  • Suspension. For a confirmed breach Own Hub may warn you; for a second breach, or for refusing to pay what you owe under this section, Own Hub may suspend your account.

8. Your account and the signed copy#

  • You agree to this agreement in the app. The app records the version, the time and the language you agreed in.
  • Then print this page, write your full name, your national ID number and the date, sign it by hand, and upload a photo or scan of it in the app. Send it only through the app, not by email or chat.
  • Own Hub staff check that the signed copy is readable and matches this version. Until they have verified it you cannot take new clients. Clients you already coach are not affected.
  • Own Hub uses your national ID number and the signed copy only to check this agreement and to prove it later. Only the staff who check it can see them. Own Hub keeps the signed copy while you coach and for 5 years after, and the Privacy Policy explains your rights over it.

9. Term and ending the agreement#

  • This agreement starts when you agree to it in the app and has no end date.
  • Either of us may end it by giving the other 30 days’ written notice: you through the app or at support@own-hub.app, Own Hub through the app or by email.
  • Own Hub may end it at once, or suspend your account, if you give false information, put a client’s safety at risk, break section 6 or 7, or break this agreement and do not put it right within 14 days of a written notice.
  • Coachings a client has already paid for continue until the end of the period she paid for, unless Own Hub decides otherwise to protect her. The commission stays due on them.

10. What continues after it ends#

  • Commissions and bills for payments received before or after the end, and for coachings still running.
  • Confidentiality and the ban on contact details (section 6).
  • The 12-month rule, the conversion fee and the agreed compensation (section 7).
  • The licence for videos already approved, as set out in section 5.
  • The Governing law and courts section (section 12), and Own Hub’s right to keep records the law requires.

11. Breach and notice#

  • If either of us breaks this agreement, the other may send a written notice saying what is wrong. The one who broke it has 14 days to put it right, unless the breach cannot be put right.
  • The rights in sections 4 and 7 apply without waiting for that notice.

12. Governing law and courts#

  • This agreement is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt, and disputes go to the competent courts in Cairo, including the Cairo Economic Court for the matters the law assigns to it. This does not take away your right to bring a claim before any other Egyptian court the law allows you to use.

13. Changes, language and contact#

  • If we change this agreement in a way that matters, there is a new version number. You accept it in the app, and Own Hub may ask for a new signed copy.
  • This agreement is written in English and Egyptian Arabic. If they differ, the Arabic applies.
  • Contact: support@own-hub.app · Mahmoud Reda, Egypt
