The short version#

  • Own Hub is a fitness app for women in Egypt aged 18 and over. Membership is for women only, for women’s safety and privacy. To keep it that way, we check every member once, when she joins: usually with her Egyptian national ID card and a live selfie, sometimes by a woman on our team instead (sections 3.3 and 3.4).
  • The photographs of your card and your face are never stored. Our server in Frankfurt, Germany reads them in memory and discards them when the check ends. We keep the result for 180 days. When your ID number is read, we also keep a keyed code made from it, so that one card cannot open two accounts.
  • Your cycle, check-ins, measurements, weight, pregnancy or postpartum stage and allergies stay on your phone, unless you turn on health backup yourself. The one other exception: if you ask to join a coach, the answers you choose to send her, such as your allergies, your life stage, your answers to seven health questions and, only if you tick a separate box, the numbers from your InBody sheet, are stored on our server in Frankfurt and shown to that coach (section 3.16).
  • Once you are verified, your workouts and meals are copied to Google Cloud in Frankfurt, so a new phone does not mean starting again. No other member can see them, unless you choose to share a dish (section 3.13), which is shared without your name.
  • If you choose to apply to coach, your application, with its documents and photos, is kept on our server in Frankfurt. Only Own Hub’s woman reviewer sees all of it, and members see only the public portfolio you build (section 3.15).
  • If you join a coach, your chat with her and the plans she shares are stored on our server in Frankfurt, readable by the two of you. Own Hub’s team reads a chat only after one of you reports it (sections 3.16 and 3.17).
  • If you pay a coach, you pay her directly by InstaPay. Own Hub never holds or moves the money. We keep a record of each payment, the transaction number you give and the screenshots you send, so your coach can confirm it and our team can decide a dispute (section 3.18).
  • To send your sign-in code, your phone number goes to Google Firebase in the United States.
  • We do not sell your data. There are no adverts and no tracking. Usage statistics and crash reports are each sent only if you allow them, and never include your health information. Usage statistics go to Google Analytics and PostHog, including in the United States.
  • We never use your data to train AI, ours or anyone’s.
  • You can look around parts of the app before you sign up, and what you enter then stays on your phone (section 3.1).
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time, and ask us for a copy of your data.

2. Who can use Own Hub, and why it is for women only#

Own Hub is for women aged 18 or over who hold an Egyptian national ID card. It is a women-only space for one reason: so that women in Egypt can train, follow their bodies and their cycles, and look after their health somewhere they feel safe and their privacy is protected. This reflects the Constitution’s protection of women, of private life, of health and of everyone’s right to sport (Articles 11, 18, 57, 59 and 84). It is not a judgement about anyone else.

When your ID card is checked, your sex and your age are read from the digits of your national ID number. We never guess them from your face. A woman can also be approved by a woman on our team, as section 3.4 describes.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If the check shows that you are under 18, your application is refused. The refusal is kept as section 6 describes.

3. What we collect, why, and on what basis#

Each basis below is one that Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law allows (Law 151/2020, Articles 2, 6 and 12).

3.1 Your account#

  • Before you sign up. You can use parts of Own Hub without an account: Home, workouts with their videos, food search and recipes. What you enter then, such as your setup answers, stays on your phone and is not sent to our servers. If you try something that needs an account, such as starting a workout or logging a meal, the app keeps it on your phone and finishes it once you are verified.
  • Your mobile number. Used to create your account and sign you in with a code sent by SMS, and to phone you if you ask a woman reviewer to check you.
  • An account identifier we generate. Used to link your data to your account.
  • Your language, settings and setup answers. This covers your privacy switches, notification preferences and quiet hours, your goal, where you train, your equipment, your training days and minutes, and your dietary preferences. Used to run the app the way you set it.
  • Basis: performing our agreement with you (the Terms of Use).

3.2 Your agreements#

When you sign up, we record which versions of the Terms of Use and this policy you accepted, in which language, from which app version, and when. We keep a similar record for each separate consent you give: the identity check, health backup and a review by a woman.

  • Basis: a legal obligation. The Executive Regulations of the Personal Data Protection Law require us to keep a record of each consent, with its date and form.

3.3 Checking that you are a woman aged 18 or over#

This happens once, when you join, and only after you have read a separate screen and agreed to it. The camera does not start until our server has recorded that agreement. For this check the camera is the only way to take the photographs: the app cannot upload a picture that is already on your phone.

  • Photographs of the front and back of your ID card. Sent over an encrypted connection to our server in Frankfurt, Germany. Google Cloud Vision reads the text on the card through its European Union endpoint. The images are not stored anywhere.
  • A short live selfie, taken while you follow prompts chosen by our server. It checks that a live person is present and that the face matches the portrait on the card. The comparison runs on our own server, with no outside face recognition service. We do not store the selfie or any face measurement.
  • Your national ID number. Used to confirm your sex (from the digit the Civil Status authority sets in the number) and your age (from the birth date encoded in it). It is then turned into a one-way keyed code, with a secret key held separately in Google Secret Manager. The number itself is not stored.
  • Your name and date of birth as printed on the card. Read only to cross-check the number, and not stored.
  • The result: approved, refused, or sent to a woman reviewer. It comes with reason codes, coarse score bands (never raw scores), the attempt number and the dates. The app shows you whether your check is pending, approved, refused or ready to try again.
  • Fraud signals: a count of failed attempts for each ID code; and a scrambled code for the app installation, together with the last ID codes tried from it, so that one installation trying many cards can be noticed.
  • Basis: your explicit consent, given in writing on the screen before the camera opens. The face match is biometric data, which the law treats as sensitive. Because a face match is sensitive data, the law also requires a permit from the Personal Data Protection Centre. Own Hub is applying for this permit under Law 151/2020, and will show its number in section 1 once the Centre issues it.
  • You can refuse. If you do, you can still ask a woman reviewer to check you (section 3.4). Without a check you cannot open a verified account, because a women-only membership is the purpose of the service.

3.4 Review by a woman#

Your case goes to a woman reviewer in four situations: the automatic check is unsure; you wear niqab and prefer a woman reviewer; you decline the camera; or the check cannot run for a technical reason.

  • Your request. When you ask for this, we record your request and the route you chose (section 3.2).
  • The queue. The review queue holds reason codes and score bands. It never holds a photograph or your phone number.
  • The contact. A woman from Own Hub reviews your case. When she is ready to contact you, she looks up your number at that moment. Each lookup is recorded with the reviewer and the time. She usually phones you within 72 hours of your request, or at the time you book, to finish your check with you. There is no video call for this check.
  • A time you book. You can book a time for the call from the free times a reviewer offers. We keep the reviewer, the time and when you booked it. The booking is deleted when you are approved, when you delete your account, or automatically 30 days after the booked time.
  • A reviewer you know. If you choose a reviewer under “I know one of the reviewers”, we note on your account which reviewer you chose, so that your request goes to her.
  • How a reviewer approves you. A woman reviewer can approve you after she has talked to you, or without a call, for example when she knows you. An Own Hub administrator can also approve an account, for example when no reviewer is available. We record how you were approved and when, and who approved you.
  • A number approved before you join. A reviewer can add a phone number to a pre-approved list before that woman signs up. When you sign in with a number on the list, you are approved without the ID check, unless your check was refused or your approval taken back before, and the number is removed from the list. Until it is used or a reviewer removes it, it is kept on our server, where only the Own Hub team can see it. To have a number removed, write to info@own-hub.app.
  • Without your ID number. If you are approved by a reviewer or from the list and your ID number was never read, no keyed code of an ID number is kept for your account.
  • Nothing is recorded. The check is not recorded, and the reviewer writes down only her decision and the date.
  • Your ID number. If your case has no ID code yet, the reviewer may ask you to read out your ID number. It is turned into the keyed code at once, and is never stored or written to a log.
  • Our team’s alert. When a case enters the queue, our team gets an alert containing only the case number and the reason codes. It is sent through a team chat service.
  • Basis: your consent, given when you ask for the review. For a number added to the pre-approved list before you join, the basis is your request to the reviewer to let you join this way; the number is used only for that and removed once it is used.

3.5 Your training and meals#

Workouts, sets, weights and repetitions, weekly goals, personal bests, the meals you log, your usual plates, your protein target and your meal plans stay on your phone. Once your account is verified, a copy is also kept in Google Cloud Firestore in Frankfurt, Germany, so that it survives a lost or replaced phone. Server security rules let only your own verified account read it. No other member can see it, unless you choose to share a dish (section 3.13), which is shared without your name.

Your Ramadan setting stays on your phone only.

If you add an Own Hub widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, it shows how many sessions you have done this week, whether today’s workout is done and whether you have logged a meal, and nothing else. The widget reads this from your phone. It never shows your cycle, pregnancy or postpartum stage, weight, measurements or allergies, and nothing is sent anywhere. Signing out or deleting your account clears it.

Your profile photo stays on your phone. Own Hub never uploads it. Only you see it. Delete everything removes it from this phone; if you back up your whole phone, a copy made earlier stays in that backup.

The workout card you can share is made on your phone from your session, its calories are an estimate, and nothing is sent unless you tap Share and choose where it goes; there is no account and no tracking involved.

  • Basis: performing our agreement with you.

3.6 Your health information stays on your phone#

By default, these are kept on your phone only: your cycle settings and period days; your daily check-ins (for example energy, sleep, bloating, pain and bleeding); your body measurements and weight; your life stage (pregnancy, postpartum or training around an injury); and the allergies you tell us about.

You can choose to back them up. In Profile, then Privacy, Back up my health data asks for a separate consent. That consent names this information and the countries where it would be stored. Nothing is copied until our server has recorded the consent, and our server’s security rules refuse the copy without it. If you turn backup off, we delete the copy on our servers straight away. The copy on your phone stays.

How you say you feel after a session and your answers on the Friday week review stay on this phone. They are never part of the backup, even when health backup is on, they are never sent for statistics, and Delete everything erases them.

The exception: a coach you ask to join. If you ask to join a coach, the answers you choose to send with your request are stored on our server in Frankfurt, Germany, and shown to that coach: your goal, level, training days and where you train, your life stage, the allergies you keep on the form, your answers to the seven health questions, whether you have spoken to your doctor, your note, your first name if you give it and, only if you tick a separate box for them, the numbers you confirmed from your InBody result sheet (section 3.16). This is separate from health backup, and nothing else from your phone is sent with it.

An InBody result sheet is read on your phone. If you choose a photo or screenshot of your sheet in Personalise in Eat, the app reads it on the phone, and the picture is never uploaded and never kept. You check each number before it is used. The weight and height you confirm stay on your phone with your other body measurements.

Your cycle and health information is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never sent to any analytics service, and never used to train AI.

  • Basis: your explicit consent, given in writing in the app, which the law requires for health data. The law also requires a permit from the Centre, which Own Hub is applying for (section 3.3).

3.7 Technical data#

  • Sign-in security. When you sign in, Google Firebase Authentication receives your IP address and device details. To stop abuse of text messages, Firebase may confirm that the request comes from a real device, using Google Play Integrity, an Apple push notification, or a reCAPTCHA check page.
  • Genuine-app check. From the first time you open the app, Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest (iOS) to confirm that requests come from the real Own Hub app. Firebase gives the installation a random identifier for this.
  • Selfie guidance. Google ML Kit detects your face on the phone to guide the selfie camera. Your images do not leave the phone through ML Kit. ML Kit sends Google the device model, operating system, app version, an installation identifier and performance events.
  • Crash reports, only if you allow them. You can allow them on the sign-up screen, or later in Profile, then Privacy. They are off until you do. What Firebase Crashlytics receives when the app crashes: the type of error and where in the code it happened, the screen it happened on (shown as “private” for screens about your body or your cycle), your device model, operating system, app version, free memory and storage, and an installation identifier. It also receives a report when one of our servers fails in a way that is our fault, with only the error’s status code. If you have also turned on usage statistics, the last screens and steps you used are shown with the report. What is never attached: your account identifier, your name, your phone number, or the text of any error. If you turn them off, reports waiting on your phone are deleted.
  • Usage statistics (“Help improve Own Hub”), only if you allow them. A separate switch on the sign-up screen and in Profile, then Privacy. Off until you turn it on. When it is on, Google Analytics for Firebase receives which screens you open (by a short screen code such as “T01”) and which steps you reach: the tour, each sign-up step, whether the ID check started, went to a reviewer, asked for new photos, let you in or was refused (one word, never why), your first workout and meal, reaching your weekly target, coming back after a week or more away, and your language and kitten settings. Google adds a random app instance identifier, your device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate location it works out from your IP address. It never receives your health information, cycle, body measurements, weight, life stage, pregnancy, allergies, name, phone number, ID number, anything you type, or the names of recipes or programmes. We do not give it your account identifier and do not link what it receives to your account. We never use it for advertising: there is no advertising identifier, and advertising uses are switched off. If you turn it off, collection stops at once and the identifier on your phone is reset, so what was sent before cannot be joined to anything sent later.
  • PostHog, with the same switch. When usage statistics are on, PostHog, in the United States, also receives a few app milestones: starting and finishing the tour, starting and finishing sign-up, signing in, starting and finishing a workout, and sending a coaching request. With them come a random identifier for this installation and technical details the PostHog library adds, such as your device model, operating system and app version. Each time the app starts, it also sends one fixed technical line that says only that statistics are working. We send PostHog no account identifier, health information, messages or payment details. For the milestones we ask it not to work out your location from your IP address. It builds no profile of you, and session recording is off. If you turn usage statistics off, collection stops and the identifier is reset. What PostHog already received is not linked to your account, and it is kept as section 6 says.
  • Our server logs. These record outcomes and reason codes. They never record your phone number or your ID number, and they never place your account identifier beside an outcome.
  • Basis: keeping the service secure and working, which the law allows (Law 151/2020, Article 6, point 4), except crash reports and usage statistics, which each need your consent. ML Kit runs only on the selfie screen, after you have agreed to the check.

3.11 Reminders and notifications#

Most notifications Own Hub shows are made on your phone and never come from our servers. Two kinds come from our server: word that your account check is done, and coaching notifications.

  • Scheduled on your phone. Training reminders, rest-timer alerts, the “add a session you did” nudge and the gentle check-ins after some days away are all scheduled by the app on your phone. They are never sent from our servers, and we do not receive anything about them.
  • The words are chosen on your phone. The words of a reminder are chosen on your phone from what you did in the app, such as your training days, the food you log and which notes you open, and nothing about that choice is sent anywhere.
  • Event reminders. When Own Hub events are open to you, the app reads the list of events and, for the next few, whether you booked a seat. It then schedules a few notes on your phone: three days, one day and three hours before an event you have not booked, and the morning of and three hours before an event you have booked. The notes do not name the event. They are scheduled on your phone, never sent from our servers, and we receive nothing about them. You can turn them off with Event reminders in Notifications. Quiet hours apply.
  • What we store for push. If you ask to be told when your check is done, we store a device token for this phone. We use it to tell you that your check is done and, if you use coaching, to send the coaching notifications below to this phone. We use it for nothing else. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Coaching notifications. If you apply to coach or use coaching, our server sends notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging to a phone registered for them: news about your coach application or your portfolio photos, a new coaching request, an answer to a request, a coaching that starts, ends soon or has ended, a new message, a shared plan, a proposed live session and news about a payment (section 3.18). The text on your lock screen is one fixed, general line, such as “You have a new message.”, and never contains a message, a name, a plan or an amount. They are sent when the event happens, at any time of day.
  • Quiet hours. Reminders scheduled on your phone are never shown between 22:00 and 08:00. Anything due in that time waits until 08:00. Three things can still reach you then: a rest timer you started yourself, word that your account check is done, and coaching notifications.
  • Your choice. You can turn each type of reminder off in Notifications in the app. Notifications from our server have no switch in the app: to stop them, turn off notifications for Own Hub in your phone’s settings, which turns off all the others too.
  • What they never show. Notifications never show health, cycle or weight details.

3.12 Meal photos#

Once your account is verified, you can photograph a meal and Own Hub will estimate what is on the plate. This is optional. You can always type your meal instead.

  • When a photo is sent. Only when you tap “Analyse my meal”, after you have seen the photo. Nothing is sent while the camera is open, and nothing is sent on its own.
  • Where it goes. To Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) in the European Union, in Frankfurt, Germany (europe-west3). The photo is sent once, without its location or other details from your camera, and is used only to estimate the meal.
  • Not kept. Own Hub does not keep the photo, on your phone or on our servers, and never puts it in your photo library. To take it, the camera writes a temporary copy on your phone for a moment, and the app deletes that copy right away. The photo is then held only in the app’s memory, and is deleted as soon as the estimate comes back. Our server logs record only the outcome and how many foods were found, never the photo or the food. Separately from the logs, see the next point.
  • A repeat answer, kept 30 days. To answer the same photo again without asking Google again, our server keeps a fingerprint of the photo (a one-way code made from the photo, from which the photo cannot be rebuilt) and the foods and weights found in it, for 30 days, then deletes them automatically. It holds no photo, and nothing that identifies you: no account identifier, name or phone number. It is used only when the exact same photo is analysed again.
  • Not used for training. Under Google Cloud’s terms, Google does not use the photo to train its models, and we never do either.
  • An estimate you check. The foods and their weights are a guess. The energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fibre figures come from public nutrition tables (USDA FoodData Central) and a table of Egyptian dishes, never from the AI. You can change every item, its weight and its figures, remove it or add another, and nothing is logged until you tap “Log this meal”. What you log is kept with your meals (section 3.5).
  • A limit. Up to 5 meal photos a day for each member.
  • Your agreement. Before your first meal photo, the app asks for your agreement and our server records it, with the date, the language and the app version. If you do not agree, the camera does not open.
  • Basis: your consent, given in the app before your first meal photo.

Saying a meal#

On the Eat screen you can tap the microphone and say what you ate instead of typing it. This is optional. You can always type your meal instead.

  • When your phone listens. Only after you tap the microphone on the Eat screen, or after you have tapped it once before. Your phone asks for the microphone, and on iPhone for speech recognition, the first time. Nothing listens in the background, and it stops when you tap stop, pause, or leave the screen.
  • Who turns your words into text. Your phone’s own speech service, not Own Hub: Apple Speech on iPhone, and the speech recogniser of your Android phone (usually Google’s). Where your phone has the language downloaded, this happens on the phone itself. Where it does not, Apple or Google may process what you say on their servers to turn it into text, under their own privacy terms. Own Hub asks for the phone to do it on the phone first, and has no control over what Apple or Google do when it cannot.
  • What Own Hub receives and keeps. Only the text, on your phone, to find the foods in it. Own Hub does not record, receive or keep the audio, and does not send the text to its servers. What it keeps is the foods you confirm, as for a meal you typed.
  • You check it first. The words appear on the screen, you can fix any word, and nothing is logged until you tap “Add it”. What you log is kept with your meals (section 3.5), in the same way as a meal you typed.
  • Without a connection. If your phone cannot turn speech into text without the internet, the app says so and you can type your meal instead.
  • Basis: your consent, given when you allow the microphone and speech recognition on your phone.

3.13 Sharing a dish with other members (optional)#

Under the meal check, and in Profile, then Privacy, there is a switch that lets you help other members find Egyptian dishes. It is off until you turn it on, and it is separate from the meal photo agreement.

  • What is sent. Only when the switch is on and you tap “Log this meal”: the name of the dish or dishes, what is in it (the names of the foods and their weights) and the energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat figures. It goes to a list on our server that only the Own Hub team can read. It never includes your photo or your name.
  • How we count you once. To avoid counting your confirmation twice, the server keeps a marker made from your account identifier with a secret key that only the server holds, so the marker cannot be turned back into you by anyone without that key. The marker is deleted when you delete your account. A dish waiting for review that only you had confirmed is deleted with it.
  • Review. The Own Hub team reviews each dish before anyone sees it.
  • What other members see. A dish the team approves is shown to all verified members, with its name, category, allergens and figures. It does not show what is in it, your photo or your name, and it carries no link to you.
  • Turning it off. Turning the switch off stops any future sharing. It does not withdraw dishes that have already been approved, because they carry no link to you and we cannot tell which are yours.
  • Basis: your consent, given by turning the switch on.

3.14 Event seats#

If you reserve a seat at an Own Hub event, our server records your place: your account identifier, when you reserved and the status of the seat, under that event. Only you and the Own Hub team can read it. The team uses it to count seats and run the event. Cancelling your seat deletes it, and so does deleting your account.

  • Basis: performing our agreement with you.

3.15 If you apply to coach (optional)#

This applies only if you choose to apply, from Profile, then Become a coach, and only if you are a verified member. If you never apply, none of it happens. Coaches are checked before members can see them. In this version an approved coach has a public portfolio that members can read, and members can join a coach (section 3.16). A member may pay her coach directly, as section 3.18 describes.

What an application holds:

  • your full name, used only for the review;
  • your display name, governorate, languages, a one-line headline and your profile photo;
  • your education (degree, institution and year) and your certificates (title, issuer and year), each with a photo of the document if you add one;
  • your specialties, years of experience and qualifications;
  • at least two pieces of feedback from clients: the words, who it is from as you typed it, and a screenshot if you add one;
  • at least one before and after transformation, with the photos of your client and your confirmation that she agreed in writing;
  • optionally, photos of yourself or your work;
  • how you coach: your approach, check-ins each week, how quickly you reply and where you train;
  • the version of the coach terms you accepted, and when. You accept them in the last step, and the Coach Terms are published on this site.

Until you send it, your draft stays on your phone, and Delete everything erases it. You pick each picture with the camera or your phone’s own picker, and Own Hub receives only the pictures you choose. When you send the application they are uploaded to Google Cloud Storage in Frankfurt, Germany, in a folder that only your account can write to. Feedback screenshots and before and after photos show other people, so add them only with the written consent of the woman in them. Photos of yourself or your work are yours to choose.

The reviewer checks every picture before members see it. When she approves one, Own Hub keeps a re-encoded copy with no location or other metadata and deletes the original. You can remove any item from your portfolio yourself, and all of it is deleted with your account.

Who sees what:

  • Own Hub’s woman reviewer sees the whole application, including feedback screenshots and transformation photos, to decide it.
  • The Own Hub administrator can read the written parts of the application and open your profile photo, education documents and certificates. Before an item is approved, only you and Own Hub’s woman reviewer can open your feedback screenshots and before and after photos. Once approved, an item is shown to verified members on your portfolio like the rest of it.

3.16 Joining a coach, and chatting with her#

This applies only if you ask to join a coach, from her portfolio. Asking to join is free. If your coach accepts you and charges for her package, you pay her directly (section 3.18).

  • What you share with her. When you ask to join, you fill in a short form: your goal, your level, how many days a week, where you train, the allergies you choose to keep on it (they come filled in from your profile and you can remove them), your life stage, your answers to seven health questions, whether you have spoken to your doctor, and a note if you write one. Your first name, if you give it, goes to the coach you ask, together with your answers. Our server stores it with the request, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Your InBody sheet (optional). In the form you can choose a photo or screenshot of your InBody result sheet. The app reads it on your phone. The picture is not uploaded and not kept: the app deletes the copy it makes as soon as the reading ends. You check every number, and you can type the numbers instead. Only the numbers you confirm are sent, and only if you tick a separate box for them: weight, body fat percentage, skeletal muscle mass and metabolic rate. They are stored with your other answers in Frankfurt, Germany, shown to that coach under the same rules, deleted with the coaching, and included in your data export. Your coach’s app can use them to suggest a calorie and protein starting point. It suggests none while you are pregnant or after a birth, or if your answers about food or weight ask her to guide your food herself.
  • Who sees it. Only that coach, and only while the coaching lasts. When it ends, she loses access to it. Nobody else sees it. You can still read it, and it is kept until you or the coach delete your account.
  • Chat. Once the coach accepts, you and she can exchange text messages. They are stored on Google Cloud Firestore in Frankfurt, readable by both of you. Own Hub staff read a thread only after one of you reports it. When the coaching ends the chat closes, and the messages stay until either of you deletes her account, which deletes the coaching and its messages (a reported message is kept as section 3.17 describes). Messages that contain a phone number, payment details, a link or abusive words are blocked: the app stops them before they are sent, and if one reaches our server, the server hides it and erases its text.
  • Plans a coach shares. A workout plan or meal plan she shares with you is stored under your account, so you can follow it in the app.
  • Live sessions. If her package includes live sessions, she proposes a time and a link to a video call in Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams, and you confirm or decline. Own Hub has no video of its own: the session opens in that service, outside the app, under that service’s own privacy terms, and Own Hub receives nothing from the call. With the coaching we store the time, its length, the link, its status and which of you marked it done. You can read them, and the coach can while the coaching lasts.
  • Reminders. When she writes, we may send you a push notification through Firebase Cloud Messaging, at most once every few minutes for a conversation. It does not show the message (section 3.11).
  • Report and block. Every coaching chat has Report and Block, and a coach’s portfolio has Report (section 3.17).
  • Basis: your consent, given on the form when you ask to join, and performing the coaching you asked for.

Voice notes, photos and videos in coaching chat#

3.17 Reports and blocks#

You can report a coach’s portfolio or a message, and you can block the coach or the client you are talking to.

  • What a report holds. Who made it and who it is about, by account identifier; what was reported; the reason you chose from a list; any note you write, up to 1,000 characters; and the date, your language and the app version. It also keeps a copy of what was reported as it was at the time: the text of the message, or the portfolio name and headline.
  • Who sees it. Only the Own Hub team. The person you report is never told who reported her. You can follow your own reports in the app, under Reports you made.
  • What the team does. The team reads the report and, for a chat, the thread it is about. It may hide content or suspend an account (Terms of Use, section 8).
  • How long. No deletion period is set. If you delete your account, the reports you made or that are about you are kept for the safety of other members, with your account identifier replaced by a random code wherever it names you, including in the link to what was reported. If you are the woman who reported and you delete your account, the copy of what was reported is deleted. If you are the woman reported and you delete your account, the copy is kept for the safety case with your name, picture and account links removed; the words that were reported stay. No period is set for that copy yet.
  • A block. It is kept on your account: whom you blocked, when, and from where. The person you block is never told. It stops new messages, requests and sessions between you. It stays until you unblock her or delete your account.
  • Basis: keeping members safe and the service working, which the law allows (Law 151/2020, Article 6, point 4).

3.18 Paying a coach by InstaPay#

This applies only if you pay a coach, or you are a coach and a member pays you. Own Hub never holds, receives or moves the money. The member sends it by InstaPay, from her bank app, to the coach’s own InstaPay address. Own Hub shows who to pay and how much, records the payment, asks the coach to confirm that it arrived, and decides a dispute if there is one.

  • What we keep about a payment. The member and the coach, by account identifier; the package; the amount, which has a few extra piasters so that it matches only this payment; a reference code; the status and the time of each step; the coach’s InstaPay address and account name as they were when you paid; the InstaPay address you give as your own, so that your receipts can be matched; the transaction number you give; and the notes and reasons either of you writes in a dispute.
  • Screenshots. If you send screenshots of your bank’s receipt, the app reads the text on your phone to fill in the form. You can hide lines (card numbers, balances) and crop before anything is sent: only that copy leaves your phone, never the original. Our server stores the copy privately, reads it again with Google Cloud Vision in the European Union, keeps the text fields it found, and keeps a fingerprint of the picture so that the same picture cannot be used twice.
  • Checks. To limit fraud, the app on your phone and then our server compare what your proof shows with the payment: the amount, who was paid, whether the bank says it succeeded, the time, whether the transaction number or the picture was used before, and signs that the picture was edited. The result only decides whether short access to the coaching starts while the coach checks her bank. It does not prove a payment. When something does not match, a person at Own Hub decides, with a written reason. When a coach says a payment did not arrive, the bank list she attaches is checked the same way.
  • Who sees it. The member and her coach. Own Hub’s team opens a payment when it is in dispute or flagged, and looks at a coach’s first few payments, her address changes and her fee statements to keep payments safe. A woman reviews any case that holds a woman’s private data. Another coach never sees it. A coach does not see your phone number. Your own InstaPay address, if you use one, is shown on the receipt your bank makes, so we suggest an address with an @ rather than your phone number.
  • Why. So the coach can confirm a payment, our team can decide a dispute, we can prevent fraud, and we meet our legal duties. No check can prove a payment: only the coach’s bank knows. We reduce fraud; we cannot remove it. We may report fraud to the authorities and keep the evidence for that.
  • Basis: carrying out the coaching you asked for, and keeping members safe and the service working, which the law allows (Law 151/2020, Article 6, point 4).
  • Refunds and disputes. A member can ask for a refund once in the first 14 days. The request, the coach’s answer, and the notes and decision in any dispute are kept with the payment.
  • If you are a coach. Your InstaPay address and account name, your payments and your monthly fee statement (what you owe Own Hub, and when you say you paid it) are kept. A new address takes effect after 24 hours, and the members with a payment waiting are told.
  • Not in statistics. No amount, reference, transaction number or screenshot is ever sent to usage statistics or crash reports.
  • Strikes. A payment proof that staff find false is recorded as a strike on the account, and so is a false claim by a coach that she was not paid. Two strikes in 90 days remove the ability to pay or to take payments by this method. A decision is made by a person, with a written reason.
  • InstaPay and your bank handle the transfer under their own terms. Own Hub cannot undo a transfer. If you sent money to the wrong account, contact your bank at once.

9. Security#

  • Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your phone. There is no password to steal.
  • Data travels over encrypted connections, and Google encrypts it where it is stored.
  • Server rules let your account read only your own data, and the app cannot mark itself as verified. Only our server can do that.
  • We never write verification images to disk, logs or storage.
  • The secret key behind the ID code is held in Google Secret Manager, separate from the database.
  • Access to our systems is limited to the people who run Own Hub, and every lookup of a phone number for a review is recorded.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Centre within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell you within three working days after that, by SMS or email.
  • The publisher, who runs Own Hub’s systems, can technically reach stored data. He opens an individual account’s data only to answer your request, to fix a fault, to investigate misuse, or when the law requires it.
