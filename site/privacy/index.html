The short version#

  • Own Hub is a fitness app for women in Egypt aged 18 and over. To keep it women only, we check your Egyptian national ID card and a live selfie once, when you join.
  • The photographs of your card and your selfie are never stored. They are read in memory on our server in Frankfurt, Germany, and discarded when the check ends. We keep the result, and a one-way keyed code made from your ID number so that one card cannot open two accounts.
  • Your cycle, periods, daily check-ins, measurements and weight, pregnancy or postpartum status, injuries and allergies stay on your phone. They are copied to our servers only if you turn on health backup yourself, after a separate consent. Turning it off deletes the copy.
  • Your workouts and meals are copied to Google Cloud servers in Frankfurt once your account is verified, so you do not lose them if you lose your phone. Only you can read them.
  • Your phone number is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which runs in the United States.
  • We do not sell your data, show no adverts, use no advertising or analytics tools, and never use your health information for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and your data from inside the app at any time.

2. Who can use Own Hub#

Own Hub is for women aged 18 or over who hold an Egyptian national ID card. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Your age and sex are read from your national ID number during the check. If the check shows that you are under 18, your application is refused.

3. What we collect, and why#

3.1 Your account#

  • Your mobile number, to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code sent by SMS.
  • An account identifier we generate, to link your data to your account.
  • Your language and settings (privacy switches, notification preferences and quiet hours), to run the app the way you set it.

3.2 Checking that you are a woman aged 18 or over#

This happens once, when you join, and only after you have read a separate screen and agreed to it. Nothing about the camera starts before that agreement is recorded on our server. The camera is the only way to take the photographs: the app cannot upload a picture that is already on your phone.

  • Photographs of the front and back of your ID card. Sent over an encrypted connection to our server in Frankfurt, Germany. The text on the card is read by Google Cloud Vision through its European Union endpoint. We do not store the images anywhere.
  • A short live selfie, taken while you follow prompts chosen by our server. It is used to check that a live person is present and that the face matches the portrait on the card. The comparison runs on our own server and does not use any outside face recognition service. We do not store the selfie or any face measurement.
  • Your national ID number. Used to confirm your sex (from the digit the Civil Status authority set in the number) and your age (from the date of birth encoded in it), then turned into a one-way keyed code with a secret key held separately in Google Secret Manager. The number itself is not stored.
  • Your name and date of birth as printed on the card are read only to cross-check the number. Not stored.
  • The result: approved, refused, or sent to a woman reviewer, with reason codes, coarse score bands, the attempt number and the dates. Your account shows you only whether your check is pending, approved, refused or ready to try again, never the reason codes.
  • Fraud signals: a count of failed attempts per ID code, and a scrambled identifier for the app installation, so that one installation trying many different cards can be noticed.
  • Your consent: the version of the wording you accepted, and when.

We never guess your sex from your face. The decision about sex comes from your national ID.

Legal basis: your explicit consent, given on the screen before the camera opens. You can refuse. If you refuse, you cannot join Own Hub, because a women-only membership is the purpose of the service.

3.3 Review by a woman#

If the automatic check is unsure, if you wear niqab and prefer a woman reviewer, if you decline the camera, or if the check cannot run for a technical reason, your case is reviewed by a woman in a short live video call over [to be completed: the video call service used for review calls].

  • The review queue holds reason codes and scores. It never holds a photograph, and it never holds your phone number.
  • When a reviewer is ready to call you, she looks up your number from your account at that moment. Every lookup is recorded with the reviewer and the time, so that access leaves a trace.
  • The call is not recorded. The reviewer records only her decision and the date.
  • If your case has no ID code yet (for example because you chose the niqab route), the reviewer may ask you to read your ID number during the call. It is turned into the keyed code at once and is never stored or written to any log.
  • We do not send text messages about your review. A woman reviewer phones you.

3.4 Your training and meals#

Workouts, sets, weights and repetitions, weekly goals, personal bests, meals you log and your eating preferences stay on your phone, and once your account is verified a copy is also stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Frankfurt, Germany, under your account, so it survives a lost or replaced phone. Security rules on the server mean only your own signed-in, verified account can read it.

3.5 Your health information stays on your phone#

Your cycle settings, period days, daily check-ins (for example energy, sleep, bloating, pain and bleeding), body measurements and weight, your life stage (pregnancy, postpartum or training around an injury) and the allergies you tell us about are kept on your phone only, by default.

You can choose to back them up. In Profile, then Privacy, Back up my health data asks for a separate consent that names this health information and the countries it would be stored in. Only after our server has recorded that consent is any of it copied, and our server’s security rules refuse it without that consent. If you turn backup off, we delete the copy on our servers straight away; the copy on your phone stays.

Your cycle and health information is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never sent to any analytics service.

3.6 Technical data#

  • Sign-in: Google Firebase Authentication receives your IP address and device details when you sign in, for security.
  • Genuine app check: Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest (iOS), through Firebase App Check, confirms that requests come from the real app, to stop automated abuse of the check.
  • Selfie guidance: Google ML Kit, the on-device face detection that guides the selfie camera, sends Google device model, operating system, app version, an installation identifier and performance events. Your images do not leave the phone through ML Kit.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives the error, device model, operating system and app version. No account identifier, name or phone number is attached.

3.7 The waitlist on this website#

If you join the waitlist on this website, we keep the mobile number or email address you give, the language you chose, your agreement and when you joined. We use it for one thing: to message you once when Own Hub opens, and then we delete it. If that has not happened, it is deleted automatically 180 days after you joined, a period pending counsel’s confirmation. To protect the form from abuse we also keep a keyed code made from your internet address, never the address itself, and a count of attempts from it that is deleted after two days. It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Frankfurt, Germany, and no app or browser can read it. To leave the waitlist sooner, write to mahmoud.reda.acc@gmail.com.

6. How long we keep it#

  • ID card photographs and selfie: not stored. Discarded in memory when the check ends, whatever the result.
  • The single-use selfie session: valid for five minutes, then deleted automatically.
  • Your account, settings, training, meals, any health backup and your consent records: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Verification results and review records: deleted automatically 180 days after the case is decided, or earlier if you delete your account. A case still waiting for review is kept until it is decided. The 180 days are pending counsel’s confirmation.
  • The keyed code of your ID number: see section 7.
  • The reviewer access log: which reviewer opened which case, and when. It holds no photograph, no phone number and no ID number, and it is kept as an audit trail.
  • Daily totals of verifications, with no link to any person: kept.
  • A record that a deletion was carried out, reduced to its status and dates: kept as proof that your request was honoured.
  • Backups held by Google: removed within Google’s own deletion window.

7. Deleting your account#

In the app: Profile, then Your data, then the delete option. Our server locks your sign-in first, deletes your account and the data in section 3, and deletes your sign-in record last; your phone is wiped at the same time. If anything interrupts it, it finishes automatically within the hour. The deletion page explains every step, and how to ask without the app.

Three things survive on purpose:

  • The keyed code of your ID number is closed, not deleted. The link to your account is removed, and the same card cannot open a new account. Whether a woman who deleted her account may rejoin is a question we have put to counsel.
  • Safety reports you made, or that were made about you, keep the report but replace your account identifier with a random code.
  • The record that your deletion was carried out, reduced to its status and dates.

9. Security#

  • Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your phone. There is no password to steal.
  • Data travels over encrypted connections, and Google encrypts it where it is stored.
  • Server rules mean your account can read only your own data, and the app cannot mark itself as verified. Only our server can do that.
  • Verification images are never written to disk, logs or storage by us. Our logs record outcomes and reason codes, never your phone number, your ID number or your account identifier beside an outcome.
  • The secret key behind the ID code is held in Google Secret Manager, separate from the database.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Centre within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell you within three working days after that.

10. What Own Hub does not do#

  • No adverts, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics software.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photo library, microphone, or Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Nothing that guesses your sex, age or anything else about you from your face.

11. Changes to this policy#

We will publish any change here with its date. If a change means collecting something new or using your data in a new way, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
