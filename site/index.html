Fitness for women, in Egypt

Own Hub

Own Hub is a fitness app built for women in Egypt, and only for women. You join with your Egyptian mobile number and a code sent by SMS, and you are checked once, with your national ID card and a live selfie, so that every member is a woman. The check runs on our own server in Frankfurt, Germany, and the photographs of your card and your face are never stored. Your training and meals are kept with your account, so a new phone does not mean starting again. Your cycle, check-ins, measurements and weight stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up. Own Hub is free, and there is nothing in it to buy.

Own Hub was founded by Rahma Mohsen, a woman in Egypt building for women in Egypt. There is no biography to read here and nothing to take on trust: the pages below set out exactly what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and for how long.

What is inside it#

  • Today

    Today’s session is waiting when you open the app, with a walk or a meal one tap away. What you log saves at once, and you have five seconds to undo it. Nothing here stops you to ask whether you are sure.

  • Train

    A workout player that keeps your place: tap a set to log it, rest timers that start themselves, a swap for any move that hurts or needs equipment you do not own, and a way to log a whole session from memory if you trained without your phone.

  • Eat

    Log a meal by tapping one of your usual plates, or by typing a sentence like “ful and baladi bread”. Egyptian food, under the names you already use for it.

  • My cycle

    Period and symptom tracking, private from the start. On a hard day the app offers a gentler session instead of the one it planned. Your cycle can be deleted on its own, without touching anything else.

  • Progress

    Your week, your personal bests, the milestones you have passed, and body measurements nobody else sees.

  • Kept for you, and only you

    Once you are verified, your training and meals are kept with your account in Frankfurt, and only you can read them. Your cycle and health information stays on your phone unless you back it up. Profile, then Your data, deletes your account and everything in it.

What this app will not do#

  • No daily streaks. We count weeks, not days, because a real life is not a straight line.
  • No calorie countdown following you around all day.
  • Nothing ranks women by body weight or by followers.
  • No adverts, no purchases, nothing sold.

What is not in this version. There is no community and no coaching: nothing in the app connects you to another member. There is no assistant. There is no export of your data yet, and the app says so rather than showing a button that would produce nothing.

The app is in Arabic and English, right to left included. You can change the language from Profile, then Language, at any time.
