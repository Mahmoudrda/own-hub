Fitness for women, in Egypt

Own Hub

Own Hub is a fitness app built for women in Egypt, and only for women. There is no account here: no sign-up, no password and no phone number, so the app was never told who you are. It has no server either, which means it collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere, and asks for no permission at all — the released Android build does not even ask to use the internet. Everything you log is written to your own handset so it is still there tomorrow, and one button in your profile erases all of it at once. Own Hub is free, and there is nothing in it to buy.

Own Hub was founded by Rahma Mohsen, a woman in Egypt building for women in Egypt. There is no biography to read here and nothing to take on trust: the app asks you for nothing, keeps nothing of yours, and the pages below set out exactly how.

  • No accountNo sign-up, no password, no phone number. It opens straight onto your own day.
  • No serverThere is no network library in the app. It cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No permissionsNo camera, no photos, no microphone, no location, no health data, no notifications.
  • FreeNo adverts, no purchases, no subscriptions. Nothing in it is for sale.
  • Arabic and EnglishThe whole app in both, right to left included.

What is inside it#

  • Today

    Today’s session is waiting when you open the app, with a walk or a meal one tap away. What you log saves at once, and you have five seconds to undo it. Nothing here stops you to ask whether you are sure.

  • Train

    A workout player that keeps your place: tap a set to log it, rest timers that start themselves, a swap for any move that hurts or needs equipment you do not own, and a way to log a whole session from memory if you trained without your phone.

  • Eat

    Log a meal by tapping one of your usual plates, or by typing a sentence like “ful and baladi bread”. Egyptian food, under the names you already use for it.

  • My cycle

    Period and symptom tracking, private from the start. On a hard day the app offers a gentler session instead of the one it planned. Your cycle can be deleted on its own, without touching anything else.

  • Progress

    Your week, your personal bests, the milestones you have passed, and body measurements nobody else sees.

  • It stays on your phone

    Own Hub has no server. What you log is written to your own handset, no other app can read it, and nothing uploads it. Profile, then Your data, erases every bit of it at once.

What this app will not do#

  • No daily streaks. We count weeks, not days, because a real life is not a straight line.
  • No calorie countdown following you around all day.
  • Nothing ranks women by body weight or by followers.
  • No adverts, no purchases, nothing sold.

What is not in this version. There is no community and no coaching: nothing in the app connects you to another person. There is no sign-in, no identity check and no camera. There is no assistant, and nothing you write is sent to any service. There is no export of your data yet, and the app says so rather than showing a button that would produce nothing.

The app is in Arabic and English, right to left included. You can change the language from Profile, then Language, at any time.
