What is inside it#

Want to see it first? Open the app and look around before you sign up: Home, workouts and recipes. What you enter before you sign up stays on your phone.

Coaching with Own Hub-reviewed coaches is available. Asking to join is free; a coach may charge for her own package, which you pay her directly. This version has no community or assistant, and nothing in the app connects you to another member except a coach you choose.

Women only, checked once#

A women-only space only means something if someone checks. So we check every member once, at the door, and then stay out of her way.

Whatever you note about your body, plus your measurements and weight, stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up, or to send some of your answers to a coach you ask to join. The privacy policy says exactly what the app keeps, where, and for how long.

  1. You join with your Egyptian mobile number and a code sent by SMS.
  2. Usually you show your national ID card and take a live selfie. If the check can’t decide, or you prefer it, a woman from Own Hub checks you instead.
  3. The check runs on our own server in Frankfurt, Germany. The photographs of your card and your face are never stored.
