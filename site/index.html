What is inside it#

Train, cook and follow your cycle, privately. In the app, “Show me around” shows it all on a sample week before you join, and saves nothing.

What is not in this version: there is no community and no coaching, so nothing in the app connects you to another member, and there is no assistant.

Women only, checked once#

A space for women only means something only if it is checked. So every member is checked once, at the door, and then the check stays out of her way.

Your cycle, measurements and weight stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up. The privacy policy says exactly what the app keeps, where, and for how long.

  1. You join with your Egyptian mobile number and a code sent by SMS.
  2. You show your national ID card and take a live selfie. If the check cannot decide, a woman from Own Hub contacts you to arrange it.
  3. The check runs on our own server in Frankfurt, Germany. The photographs of your card and your face are never stored.
