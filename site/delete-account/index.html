Delete your Own Hub data

Last updated: 24 September 2026

The short version#

Own Hub has no accounts and no server. We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Everything you put into the app is on your own phone. To erase it, open Own Hub, go to Profile, then Your data, then Delete everything. It happens at once.

Why there is nothing on our side#

This version of Own Hub has no server, no account and no network code at all.

  • There is no sign-up and no sign-in. The app has never been told who you are.
  • The released Android app does not even ask for permission to use the internet.
  • There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising library in it.

So we never received your training, your meals, your measurements or your cycle, and we cannot delete, correct, export or look at any of it. There is no record of you to find.

What is on your phone, and what deleting removes#

The app writes one document into your phone’s own private settings store, so that closing the app does not lose your week. Deleting removes it, along with everything the app is holding:

  • Your training: sessions, sets, weights and repetitions, and the workouts you swapped.
  • Your meals, your usual plates and your eating preferences.
  • Your weekly goal, the weeks you completed, your personal bests and your milestones.
  • Body measurements and weight you entered.
  • Your cycle: every period day you recorded, your check-ins and your cycle settings.
  • What you told the app you avoid eating, and your setup answers.
  • Your privacy switches and your language.

The app then returns to its first day and tells you so.

How to delete it#

In the app, which is the only way it needs#

  1. Open Own Hub.
  2. Tap your picture at the top of Home to open Profile.
  3. Tap Your data.
  4. Read the sheet. It says what will be removed and that it cannot be undone.
  5. Tap Delete everything.

It is immediate. There is no waiting on us, no email to send, no code to confirm, and no queue, because nothing about it involves us.

Deleting the app removes everything too. The document lives with the app, so uninstalling takes it with it.

Without installing the app, by email#

You can ask us to delete your data without installing Own Hub at all. The button below opens a message to mahmoud.reda.acc@gmail.com with the subject and the text already written; send it, change it, or write your own.

Open a ready-made deletion email

Here is the honest answer you will get back. In this version of Own Hub nothing of yours exists on any server, ours or anyone else’s, so there is nothing for us to find, delete, correct or send you. We will confirm that in writing, and point you at the steps above, which are the only deletion that has anything to delete.

We are keeping this route open anyway, because you are entitled to ask a company what it holds about you without having to install its software first, and because the day accounts arrive it becomes a real request with a real queue behind it.

If the button does not work on your device, write to mahmoud.reda.acc@gmail.com yourself. Anything you send is an ordinary email, held in an ordinary mailbox, and you do not need to include your ID, a photograph or any health information — we will never ask you for them.

It cannot be undone#

There is no restore and no grace period. Once it is done, your logs, your measurements and your cycle history are gone, and we cannot bring them back, because we never had a copy.

Downloading a copy first is not built in this version. The sheet in the app says so plainly rather than offering a button that would produce nothing, and there is nothing we could send you either.

Delete only some of it#

You do not have to delete everything to delete the part that matters to you.

  • Cycle data can be deleted on its own, from cycle settings, without touching anything else.
  • You can delete individual logs, meals and measurements as you go.

What changes when accounts arrive#

A later version of Own Hub will add accounts, identity verification and cloud storage. From that version onwards this page will describe two routes, deleting from inside the app and asking us to delete without installing it at all, and it will say what survives a deletion and why.

Nothing in that paragraph is running today. The Privacy Policy carries the full description, in Part B.

Your rights#

Under Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 you can ask a company what it holds about you, correct it, get a portable copy, have it deleted, and withdraw a consent you gave.

In this version we hold nothing about you, so there is nothing to see, correct, port or ask us to delete. Everything is on your phone and under your control.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a request, you can complain to Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Centre. [to be completed: contact details for Egypt's Personal Data Protection Centre]
