Own Hub: Google Play Data safety answers (account build, privacy policy v3)
=======================================================================

Copy these answers into Play Console > App content > Data safety. They must
match ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy, the App Store App Privacy answers and
the published privacy policy (v3, sections 3 to 5). Wording of the policy is
pending review by a licensed Egyptian lawyer. After any change, regenerate the
Xcode privacy report and compare all four line by line before submitting.

Overview
--------
Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types?  Yes
Is all user data encrypted in transit?                              Yes
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?  Yes
  In the app: Profile > Your data > Delete everything.
  On the web: https://own-hub.app/delete-account (LegalLinks.deleteAccount).
Is data shared with third parties?                                  No
  Google Cloud and Firebase, the SMS route and the chat service for review
  alerts act as service providers processing on our behalf, which Play does
  not count as sharing. Google Analytics for Firebase is a service provider
  on the same terms only while its data-sharing settings stay off, Google
  signals stay off and no Google Ads account is linked (owner action in the
  Analytics property); if any of those is turned on, this answer changes. There is no video service: a woman from Own Hub
  reviews the case and contacts her by phone.
Independent security review                                          No

Data types collected
--------------------
For every type below: Collected = yes, Shared = no. Ephemeral, required or
optional, and purposes as noted.

Personal info
  - Name
      Collected, processed ephemerally: read from the ID card in memory to
      cross-check the ID number, never stored. Required.
      Purpose: Fraud prevention, security and compliance.
  - Phone number
      Collected. Not ephemeral. Required.
      Purpose: Account management, App functionality, Fraud prevention.
      Sign-in by SMS code (Firebase Authentication), and so a woman from
      Own Hub can contact her by phone when she asks for a review.
  - User IDs
      Collected. Not ephemeral. Required.
      Purpose: Account management, App functionality.
      The Firebase uid her account and documents are keyed by.
  - Other info: national ID number, and the sex and age read from it
      Collected. Kept only as a keyed one-way code. Required.
      Purpose: Fraud prevention, security and compliance; App functionality.

Health and fitness
  - Fitness info
      Collected for verified accounts (workouts, sessions, meals, plans).
      Required. Purpose: App functionality.
  - Health info
      Collected ONLY when she turns on "Back up my health data" (off by
      default, with its own consent): cycle and period dates, daily check-ins,
      body measurements including weight, pregnancy, postpartum or injury
      stage, allergies. Optional. Purpose: App functionality.
      Otherwise this stays on the phone and is not collected.

Other user-generated content and app info (three flows to declare)
  - Scan cache: a fingerprint (hash) of a re-encoded meal photo plus the foods
      and grams found, kept 30 days, then deleted by TTL. No photo, no user
      identifier. Purpose: App functionality. Not linked to her.
  - Dish sharing (opt-in, off by default): on "Log this meal" the dish names,
      the items (names and grams) and the macros go to a server-only list for
      team review. A marker keyed by an HMAC of her uid dedupes her
      confirmation and is deleted with her account. Approved dishes (names,
      category, allergens, macros; not the items) are shown to all verified
      members with no link to her. Shared with other users: yes, only if she
      opts in, without her name.
  - Event seats: her uid, time reserved and status under the event; readable
      by her and Own Hub admins; deleted on cancel or account deletion.

Photos and videos
  - Photos
      Collected and processed ephemerally: both sides of her national ID card
      and the selfie frames, for the women-only check. Never stored.
      Required, with a woman-reviewer alternative.
      Purpose: Fraud prevention, security and compliance; App functionality.

App activity
  - App interactions
      Collected only with her consent to usage statistics ("Help improve
      Own Hub": off by default, its own switch at sign-up and in Privacy,
      separate from crash reports). Google Analytics for Firebase: which
      screens (by id) and steps she uses, from closed lists. Never health,
      body, cycle, life stage, name, number, ID or anything she typed. No
      user ID is set; turning it off resets the analytics ID on the phone.
      Optional. Purpose: Analytics.

App info and performance
  - Crash logs
      Collected only with her consent (off by default; a switch at sign-up
      and in Privacy). Firebase Crashlytics: fatal crashes, and non-fatal
      reports of a server call that failed with a bug's status code. Not
      linked to her account: no user identifier is ever set; the only custom
      keys are the screen id, app version and build channel, and the only log
      lines are fixed words. Optional.
      Purpose: Analytics.
  - Diagnostics
      ML Kit performance events, and the device details sent with a crash
      report. Required. Purpose: Analytics.

Device or other IDs
  - Device or other IDs
      Collected: Firebase installation identifier, App Check attestation, and
      a hashed install id kept for fraud signals. Required.
      Purpose: Fraud prevention, security and compliance; App functionality.
      Also the push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), only if she taps "Tell
      me when I'm in" while a woman reviewer has her check and allows
      notifications. Optional. Purpose: App functionality. It is used for
      one notification, "your check is done", and deleted with her account.
      Also the Google Analytics app instance ID, only with her consent to
      usage statistics, reset when she withdraws it. Optional. Purpose:
      Analytics. No Advertising ID: the AD_ID permission is removed from the
      merged manifest and google_analytics_adid_collection_enabled is false.

Location
  - Approximate location
      Only with her consent to usage statistics: Google Analytics derives an
      approximate location (country, and city unless granular location is
      turned off in the Analytics property) from the masked IP address. The
      app asks for no location permission and reads no location.
      Optional. Purpose: Analytics.

Not collected
-------------
Precise location, contacts, messages (the SMS code is read by the SMS
Retriever, not READ_SMS), audio, files and docs, calendar, web browsing,
financial info, in-app search history and the Advertising ID. The app has no
photo-library permission and cannot read the gallery. The one place she can
pick an existing picture is her own profile photo, through the system photo
picker, which hands the app only the single picture she chooses; the profile
photo stays on the phone, is never uploaded and is removed by Delete
everything, so it is not collected.
There is no video review. A woman from Own Hub who reviews a case contacts
her by phone, outside the app; nothing from that contact is collected by the
app.
Training reminders and event reminders are scheduled on the phone (local
notifications, opt-in; the training ones are asked after her first finished
session). The days and time she picks, and which events she booked, are read
on the phone to plan them; nothing about the notes is sent to Own Hub, and they
do not name the event. The manifest carries RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED so they
survive a restart. It also carries SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, used only for the
"Rest is over" alert while a rest timer runs (and only when the phone allows
it); reminders and event notes are inexact. The Play exact-alarm declaration
is an owner step.
The workout share card is drawn on the phone and leaves it only through the
system share sheet when she taps Share; nothing is uploaded by the app.

Health apps declaration (Play Console > App content > Health apps)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Categories: Activity and fitness; Nutrition and weight management; Period
tracking. Pregnancy and postpartum are fitness adaptations used with her
doctor's clearance (pending counsel). The app is not a medical device and
does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

Target audience
---------------
18 and over only, with Restrict Minor Access on. Verification refuses anyone
under 18.

Content rating
--------------
No user-generated content and no chat in this version.

App access
----------
The +20 Firebase test number with its fixed code, and the reviewer allow-list
(owner action), plus Review Notes.
