Own Hub: Google Play Data safety answers (account build)
=======================================================

Copy these answers into Play Console > App content > Data safety. They must
match ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy, the App Store App Privacy answers and
the published privacy policy. Wording of the policy is pending review by a
licensed Egyptian lawyer.

Overview
--------
Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types?  Yes
Is all user data encrypted in transit?                              Yes
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?  Yes
  (in the app: Profile and settings > delete account; and on the web page
   listed under LegalLinks.deleteAccount once it is published)
Is data shared with third parties?                                  No
  (Google Cloud / Firebase act as service providers processing on our behalf,
   which Play does not count as sharing)

Data types collected
--------------------
For every type below: Collected = yes, Shared = no, Processed ephemerally as
noted, Required unless noted, Purpose = App functionality (and Account
management where marked).

Personal info
  - Phone number
      Collected. Required. Purpose: Account management, App functionality.
      Used for sign-in by SMS code (Firebase Authentication).
  - User IDs
      Collected. Required. Purpose: Account management, App functionality.
      The Firebase uid her account and documents are keyed by.
  - Other info: government ID number
      Collected, NOT stored in readable form. Required for verification.
      Purpose: App functionality (fraud prevention: one card, one account).
      Only a keyed one-way hash of the national ID number is kept.

Health and fitness
  - Fitness info
      Collected for verified accounts (workouts, sessions, meals). Required.
      Purpose: App functionality.
  - Health info
      Collected ONLY if she turns on "Back up my health data" (off by default,
      with its own consent): cycle and period dates, daily check-ins, body
      measurements including weight, pregnancy, postpartum or injury stage,
      allergies. Optional. Purpose: App functionality.
      Otherwise this stays on the phone and is not collected.

Photos and videos
  - Photos
      Collected and processed ephemerally: both sides of her national ID card
      and a live selfie, for the women-only identity check. Never stored.
      Required for automatic verification (a woman-reviewer video call is the
      alternative). Purpose: App functionality (fraud prevention).

App info and performance
  - Crash logs
      Collected in release builds only (Firebase Crashlytics). Not linked to
      her account: no user identifier, custom key or log line is ever set.
      Purpose: App functionality (fixing crashes).
  - Diagnostics
      Collected with a crash report: device model, OS version, app version,
      free memory and disk at the moment of the crash. Not linked. Purpose:
      App functionality.

Device or other IDs
  - Device or other IDs
      Collected (Firebase installation identifier; App Check attestation).
      Required. Purpose: App functionality, Fraud prevention / security.

Not collected
-------------
Location, contacts, messages, audio, files and docs, calendar, web browsing,
financial info, app activity outside the above, and photos from the gallery
(the app cannot read the gallery).

Health apps declaration (Play Console > App content > Health apps)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Categories: Activity and fitness; Nutrition and weight management; Period
tracking. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or
prevent any condition.

Target audience
---------------
18 and over only. Verification refuses anyone under 18.
