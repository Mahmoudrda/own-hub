# Brag Plan: Own Hub

## What is this app?

Own Hub is a Flutter app for women in Egypt that holds strength training, food and
rest in one place, in Arabic or English, on a hand-drawn paper-and-rose interface.
The centrepiece is Rahma's own recipe book: 39 Egyptian dishes written in her
Egyptian Arabic, set on pale pink paper cards with wooden clips.

## The angle

This is a real build filmed on a real device. Nothing is mocked up. The video is a
walk through the app as a woman actually meets it, from the four setup questions to
her first week, with every tap visible. The claim it makes is small and true: the
app is easy to move around, and it speaks her language properly, right to left.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

The phone is already there, centred, warm rose paper behind it, and the first thing
that happens is a finger landing on "Feel better in my body". No logo sting, no
abstract intro. The name arrives over a working screen: **Own Hub**.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The four setup questions answered in three cuts, then the plan the app builds from
  them: "Days a week: 3 · Minutes a session: 20 · At home".
- The rest timer: a green ring counting 0:38 down with "Set 1 saved · 12 reps ·
  Undo" sitting above it. It films better than anything else in the build.
- Rahma's recipe cards scrolling past on pale pink paper with wooden clips, then one
  opening into her Arabic ingredients and steps.
- The whole app flipping to Arabic, right to left, bottom bar and all.

## Outro / punchline

The phone settles, the name comes back, and one plain line under it: **Strength,
food and rest. In Arabic and English.** No call to action, no store badges, because
the build is not on a store yet.

## User flow worth showing

Entry → key action → result, three times over:

1. Four setup questions → the program the answers build.
2. Start a session → tap "Done · 12 reps" → the rest timer starts itself.
3. Tap Log on a meal → the day fills in → the recipe book opens underneath it.

## Tone

- Preset: `app-store`
- Creative direction: a quiet product walkthrough; the app carries it, the captions
  stay out of the way
- Interpretation: one clean caption per beat, slide-and-settle motion, no zooms that
  fight the footage, no exclamation. Every claim is something visible on screen in
  that same second.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 40.25 seconds (user asked for 40)

- Accent: `#A24661` (AppColors.primary), deep rose ink
- Text: `#241A1E` (AppColors.onSurface)

- Recipe book: paper `#FBDCD9`, ink `#610904`, clip brass `#BF9A5E`, bg `#F5A6AF`
- Display font: Rubik 700 (the app's own face, bundled from `app/assets/fonts`)
- Body font: Rubik 400/500
- Hand font: La Belle Aurore (the app's handwriting layer, used once for the outro mark)
- Strongest visual element: the phone itself, centred, with real touch indicators
  landing on real buttons

## Share copy (draft)

Own Hub is built. Four questions set your week, one tap logs a set, the rest timer
starts itself, and Rahma's 39 recipes are in her own Arabic. The whole app flips
right to left.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm bed under a walkthrough, with three sparse interaction accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM), at low
  level throughout
- Music treatment: fades in over the first second, holds around 0.24, lifts slightly
  for the recipe-book beat, fades out under the closing card
- Music cue guidance: preset read from
  `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`.
  Beat grid is 1.07s + n x 0.5225s. Beat-locked moments: the built plan at 7.86s,
  the rest timer at 18.31s, the recipe book at 28.24s, the Arabic flip at 35.56s.
  Every scene start sits on a beat.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. The rose glow behind the phone breathes with the
  music's low-band energy. Nothing else responds, no bars, no particles.
- SFX posture: sparse. Three taps and one soft impact in forty seconds.
- Audio-coupled moments: the tap on the setup answer, the tap that ends the set and
  starts the rest timer, the tap that logs the meal, and one soft warm impact when
  the closing name lands.
- Restraint rule: no whoosh on every cut, no riser, nothing that makes a calm app
  sound like a sports ad.

## What is deliberately not in this video

Community, coaching, payments, the assistant and identity verification are switched
off in this build. None of them is filmed and nothing in the copy implies them.
