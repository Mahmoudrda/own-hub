# Surface brief: Own Hub, whole app (redesign)

**Scope:** every screen of the app, redesigned through a shared design system; first viewport is Today.
**Mode:** Operate. She completes a task in under a minute, often one-handed, often in Arabic.
**Must stay untouched:** brand hues, the logo mark, the icon set, all copy, navigation, data, behaviour, the 3,468 tests.
**Would feel wrong:** scrapbook, cute, generic wellness pink, a UI kit, handwriting that hurts reading.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A printed women's edition she writes in: exact type and grid underneath, a human hand of ink, pencil and marker annotating on top, kept like a training diary. It refuses the category's greeting-plus-stat-tiles-plus-card-carousel dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white page. Rose owns whole fields and every ink mark; blush is the marker highlight; sage marks done; ink sets the type. Rubik at editorial scale contrast, no serif, no mono, no eyebrow labels. Marks: overshooting underlines, hand-closed circles, ticks, arrows that point at values, pencil hatching for tone, one-irregular-corner frames, offset ink outlines. Ruqaa and a Latin hand, rarely, for notes.

STORY: She sees the one thing to do this week, sees her own week circled and ticked in her own record, and does it. Never ranked, never pressured.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Today as a cover. A small date line; this week's next session set large; the week as hand-circled segments beside it; one pencil arrow pointing at the action; the primary action as an ink-outlined button; a quiet list below.

FORM: The Annotated Edition, brief-pinned, first on the ordered list, fused with the training-diary grammar the roll assigned (index 4) for topology, controls and state. Seed key b16fd25e.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Raises from the declined challengers
- Every hand-drawn line connects two things; no stroke without a referent.
- One focal object leads each screen; everything else recedes.
- Marks follow reading direction: arrows, overshoots and leaders mirror in Arabic.
- Every state owns a mark, not only a colour: planned, under way, done, skipped by choice.
- The grid underneath never bends; imperfection lives only on the surface.
- Tone from pencil-hatch density, never gradients.
