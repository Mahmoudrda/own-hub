## All text on this row, in reading order

```text
Row 03
Two nav concepts — pick one
Same content, different shape. Concept A is the brief. Concept B buys legibility and reach with one tap of cost on a budgeted journey.
A — Five tabs
Recommended
Home
Train
Community
Eat
Coaching
Community takes the centre because it is the easiest position from either grip and the thing we want opened daily. Home is outermost on the leading edge — she lands there, so she rarely taps it. Coaching is outermost on the trailing edge because most users never buy.
✓
Every budgeted journey holds. Meal logging stays at two taps.
!
Labels drop to 12px — the only place in the product under the 15px floor.
!
78px per tab. The outer two are a real stretch on a 6.7" Android.
B — Four tabs, Eat folded into Today
Training
Food
Today
Train
Community
Coaching
Home and Eat are the same job: what am I doing today. The macro ring already lives on Home, so Today carries a Training / Food switch at the top and Eat loses its tab. Community keeps a centre-adjacent seat and 98px of width.
✓
Labels return to 15px. No exception to the type floor anywhere in the product.
✓
Four 98px targets, all comfortably inside the natural zone.
!
Logging a repeat meal becomes three taps, not two. That breaks a budget you set.
!
A segmented control is a fourth interaction pattern to teach her.
My call: ship A. A two-tap meal log is worth more than a 3px label, and nutrition earning its own tab is what tells her the food side is free and complete. If usability testing shows women missing the outer two tabs, B is the fallback and the switch is cheap.
```
