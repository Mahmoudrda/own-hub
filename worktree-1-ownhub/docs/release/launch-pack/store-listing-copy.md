### Full description (Play and App Store), 2672 / 4000

```
Own Hub is a fitness app for women in Egypt, and only for women. Every member is checked once when she joins, so the space you train in really is women only.

HOW THE WOMEN-ONLY CHECK WORKS
• Sign up with your Egyptian mobile number and a code sent by SMS.
• Photograph your Egyptian national ID card and take a short live selfie. The app reads your age and sex from your ID number and checks that your selfie matches the card.
• The photos of your card and your face are never stored. They are checked and then discarded. We keep only the result, and a one-way code made from your ID number so that one card cannot open two accounts.
• If the automatic check cannot decide, or you would rather not use the camera, you can ask a woman from Own Hub to review your case. She contacts you by phone within 72 hours. Nothing is recorded.
• Own Hub is for women aged 18 and over.

YOUR HEALTH DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• Your cycle, period dates, daily check-ins, measurements, weight, pregnancy or postpartum stage and allergies stay on your phone.
• They leave it only if you turn on "Back up my health data", which stays off until you choose it.
• Your workouts and meals are saved to your account, so you do not lose them with your phone.
• No ads and no tracking. Your health data is never sold and never used for marketing.

TRAIN
• A plan built around where you train, the equipment you have and the time you can give.
• A guided workout player with a rest timer that starts by itself.
• Swap any move that hurts or needs equipment you do not own.
• Pregnant, postpartum or training around an injury? Tell the app and it adjusts its suggestions.

EAT
• 39 recipes by Rahma Mohsen, made from Egyptian food you already know.
• Log a meal in one tap from your usual plates.
• Choose what you cannot eat from a list of 16 allergens. The app will not call a dish safe when it cannot tell.

CYCLE
• Track your period and how you feel each day.
• On a day you log severe cramps or heavy bleeding, the app stops suggesting a workout and points you toward care.
• Delete your cycle data on its own, at any time.

PROGRESS
• Your week, your personal bests, your milestones and body measurements that nobody else sees.

IN ARABIC AND ENGLISH
• The whole app works in Modern Standard Arabic and in English.

FREE
• Own Hub is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
• Delete your account and your data from inside the app at any time.

Own Hub is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise programme or making medical decisions, especially if you are pregnant, postpartum or injured.
```
