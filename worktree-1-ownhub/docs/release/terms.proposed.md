## 2. What Own Hub is

A fitness app for women in Egypt. It lets you follow workouts, log what you train and eat, set a weekly
goal, track your measurements, and record your menstrual cycle. What you log is kept on your phone. Once
your account is verified, your training and meal logs are also stored in your account on our servers.
Your health data (cycle, check-ins, measurements, weight, pregnancy or postpartum status and allergies)
stays on your phone unless you choose to turn on "Back up my health data". The Privacy Policy explains
how.

It is **free**. There are no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions in this version. If we
introduce anything paid, we will show you the price and the terms before you pay, and you will not be
charged without agreeing.

There is no community, coaching or assistant in this version.
