## 2. What Own Hub is, in this version

A fitness app for women in Egypt. It lets you follow workouts, log what you train and eat, set a
weekly goal, track measurements you enter yourself, and record your menstrual cycle.

**This version has no account.** There is nothing to sign up for, no password and no profile that
anyone else can see. Everything you put into it is written to your own phone and stays there.

**It is free, and there is nothing in it to buy.** No adverts, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions,
and no payment of any kind inside or outside the app.

**It has no community and no coaching in this version.** Nothing connects you to another person.
Section 12 describes what changes if those arrive, and they will arrive only in a later version, with
a later version of these Terms.

## 4. Own Hub is not medical advice

**Read this section even if you skip the rest.**

- Own Hub is a fitness and wellbeing app. It is **not a medical device, not a diagnosis, and not a
  substitute for a doctor.** Nothing in it is a clinical opinion about you.
- Workouts, meal suggestions, calorie and macro figures, and anything the app shows about your cycle
  are general guidance. They are not prescriptions.
- **Talk to a doctor before you start**, especially if you are pregnant or recently gave birth, have
  a heart, joint, bone or blood-pressure condition, diabetes, an eating disorder, are recovering
  from surgery or injury, or take medication that affects exercise.
- **Stop exercising if something hurts, and get medical help if you need it.** Nothing in the app can
  require you to continue. The app never penalises you for stopping or for resting.
- Where the app shows guidance about severe symptoms, such as heavy bleeding or severe pain, **it is
  telling you to seek care, not treating you.** If you think it is an emergency, contact emergency
  services or go to a hospital. Do not wait for an app.
- The app does not know your medical history. It only knows what you typed into it.

## 6. Your data, and your phone

There is no account and no server in this version, so there is nothing for you to secure with us and
nothing for us to lose.

- **What you log lives on your phone.** Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and read
  it. If that matters to you, use your phone's own lock.
- **You can erase everything at once**, from Profile, then Your data. It is immediate and it cannot
  be undone.
- **We cannot recover anything.** We never had it. If you delete it, or lose the phone, or delete the
  app, it is gone.
- Downloading a copy of your data is not built in this version, and the app says so rather than
  offering a button that produces nothing.

The Privacy Policy explains all of this in more detail.
